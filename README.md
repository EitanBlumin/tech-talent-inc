# Unnamed Consulting Sim

Diegetic simulator game built in Godot 4, where you found and manage a tech consulting company: Hire workers, initiate marketing campaigns, take on project leads and assign workers to work on them. You're also tasked with developing your workers as your most precious resource - but be careful! The more you build up your talent, the harder it gets to retain them!

Remember:

> In consulting, the people are the product
